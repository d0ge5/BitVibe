Installation finished

Restart your DAW, or rescan plug-ins, to find Ottoclone. In Logic Pro, the Audio Unit appears under Audio FX, and macOS may take a minute to validate it the first time.

If macOS blocks the plug-in or the app

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS may say it cannot verify the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Ottoclone and choose Open Anyway, then open the DAW again.

Uninstalling

Delete these items (an administrator password is needed for the first two):

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Ottoclone.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Ottoclone.component
  • /Applications/Ottoclone.app
